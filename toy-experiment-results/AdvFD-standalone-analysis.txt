QUALITY METRIC DEFINITION
Corrected_W2_squared_Rd and Corrected_Sinkhorn_divergence_Rd use 0.5*(h(P1,Q1)+h(P2,Q2)-h(P1,P2)-h(Q1,Q2)), with two independent target clouds P1/P2 and two independent generated clouds Q1/Q2.
h is exact empirical assignment mean squared Euclidean cost for W2 squared, or regularized OT with epsilon=0.1 for Sinkhorn; Sinkhorn same-cloud self terms cancel.
c=1: samples are unscaled. Both corrected estimates are signed squared-cost values; negative values are retained, with no square root or clipping.
Production counts are 50000 points per independent cloud for each global Sinkhorn solve and 5000 per cloud for empirical W2 squared; clouds are not split in half.
Independent replica seed offset=1000003. Seeds and subset indices are shared across matched Base/post evaluations. Target-target scalar baselines are cached once per metric sampling configuration in a run.
Small shadow probes and smoke runs are separately marked by sample counts; they are not full-quality estimates.
GeomLoss online/KeOps uses linear storage, not linear pairwise compute; scaling=0.9 is a numerical approximation, not an exact OT certificate.
These corrected finite-sample statistics are not certificates of population W2 or metric validity; read frozen-feature FD and mode coverage alongside them.
The whitened FD training objective is distinct from the frozen-feature FD evaluation. Positive metric gain means Base minus post-trained.
