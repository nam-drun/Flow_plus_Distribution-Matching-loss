Base is matched by schedule, solver, integration steps and evaluation seeds.
A2/A3 fields describe the initial FD gradient; snapshot-invariant positive-fraction diagnostics are moved to the shared table with branch scope.
Cancellation uses this branch solver at its separate fine reference resolution.
Raw FD_phi/FDr_phi and their absolute gains are intentionally hidden from the branch table. Report FD_phi_ratio_FD_over_base = FD_phi(FD,T)/FD_phi(Base,T) and FD_phi_relative_gain = 1 - that ratio.
Transport quality reports only corrected W2^2 and corrected Sinkhorn at c=1, their matched Base values, and Base-minus-FD gains.
Base and DAE training network forwards use BF16 autocast on supported CUDA devices (FP32 fallback); their losses and parameters remain float32. All FD first/second moments and Bures/eigendecomposition algebra are computed in float64; generator and encoder forward passes during FD post-training and evaluation remain float32.
